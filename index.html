How a job runs

Most epoxy floors fail because of what happened before the resin went down. Surface prep is where the money should go.

1

Site visit

We check moisture in the slab, cracks and how the floor is actually used. Free within our service area.

2

Surface prep

Diamond grinding or shot blasting to open the concrete, then crack and pothole repair.

3

Primer & body coat

Penetrating primer, then the build coats for your chosen system. Curing time respected between coats.

4

Top coat & handover

Final coat, line marking if needed, and a written care sheet. We tell you when to put load back on.

Questions we get asked

How much does epoxy flooring cost per sq ft?

It depends on the system and the condition of your existing floor. A basic epoxy coating and a 6 mm PU screed can differ by several times. Send photos and the area and we'll give you a band, not a vague number.

Can you coat over my existing tiles or old epoxy?

Sometimes. Tiles need mechanical keying and a check for hollow ones. Old epoxy needs testing for adhesion — if it's already lifting, it has to come off. We check this on the site visit.

How long before we can use the floor?

Light foot traffic in about 24 hours, full chemical and load resistance in about 7 days. PU screeds are usually faster. Cold weather slows curing.

Will it be slippery?

A gloss finish is slippery when wet. For wet areas, ramps and washdown zones we add an anti-skid aggregate. Tell us where water will be on the floor.

Do we have to shut the whole plant?

No. We usually work in phases or bays so one section cures while another runs. It costs a little more in mobilisation and takes longer overall.

Do you work outside Maharashtra?

Our regular area is Maharashtra. For larger projects elsewhere, ask — travel and lodging get added to the quote.
